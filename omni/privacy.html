Introduction & Core Architecture

This Privacy Policy applies to the Omni Universal AC Remote mobile application ("the App"), developed and maintained by Karyanest Softwares.

Our engineering philosophy is strictly offline-first and privacy-respecting. The App operates as a standalone utility that uses your Android phone's built-in Consumer Infrared (IR) blaster hardware to transmit control signals directly to your air conditioning unit. All signal encoding, protocol state synthesis, and remote configurations are computed locally on your device.

We do not collect, store, monetize, share, or sell any personal information or telemetry from our users.

2

Data We Do Not Collect

In accordance with Google Play's User Data policy and global privacy regulations, we explicitly declare that the following data categories are never collected, stored, or transmitted:

  • Personal Identifiers: Names, email addresses, phone numbers, user accounts, or profile data. No login or account creation is required.
  • Location Data: Precise or approximate GPS, network, or cell tower location.
  • Sensory Data: Voice recordings, camera feeds, photos, or media files.
  • Personal Content: Contacts, text messages, clipboard content, or file storage.
  • Behavioral Profiling: We do not log which buttons you press, what temperature you set, or when you use the remote.
  • Advertising Identifiers: The App contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, ad networks, or user-tracking libraries.
3

Local On-Device Storage

The App stores only basic operational settings locally on your device via standard Android SharedPreferences:

  • Selected AC brand and active remote profile index.
  • List of saved AC remotes configured by the user.
  • User interface preferences (Light/Dark theme, Celsius/Fahrenheit toggle, Haptic vibration strength).

This information is strictly sandboxed within the application directory on your device and is never uploaded to any remote server. Uninstalling the App permanently purges all stored preferences.

4

Android System Permissions

The App requests only the minimal set of operating system permissions strictly necessary to perform its functions:

📡
TRANSMIT_IR (android.permission.TRANSMIT_IR)
Enables the app to emit modulated infrared pulses via your phone's built-in IR blaster to communicate with your air conditioner.
Required
📳
VIBRATE (android.permission.VIBRATE)
Powers tactile haptic vibration when physical remote buttons are pressed. Can be adjusted or completely disabled in App Settings.
Optional
🌐
INTERNET (android.permission.INTERNET)
Used exclusively to query Google Play In-App Updates for newly released versions and to launch this Privacy Policy document in your web browser. No telemetry or personal data is transmitted.
