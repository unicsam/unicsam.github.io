Overview

Omni: Universal AC Remote ("the App") is developed by Karyanest Softwares. This app uses your phone's built-in IR blaster to control air conditioners — nothing more.

We have designed the App with a strict privacy-first approach. We do not require account creation, we do not upload your usage data, and we do not display advertisements of any kind.

2

Data We Do Not Collect

The following data is never collected, transmitted, or stored on any server:

👤
Name, email, or account information
No login or account is required to use the App.
📍
Location data
The App never requests or accesses your location.
📋
Contacts, messages, or clipboard
The App does not access any of your personal content.
🎙️
Microphone or camera
No audio or visual data is captured.
📊
Usage analytics or behaviour profiling
We do not track which buttons you press or how often you use the App.
3

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All preferences you configure in the App (saved AC remotes, theme selection, temperature unit, and vibration strength) are stored exclusively on your device using Android's SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your phone.

Uninstalling the App will permanently delete all locally stored preferences.

4

Crash Analytics

The App uses anonymous crash reporting (via Google Play's built-in Android Vitals) to help us identify and fix stability issues. This data:

• Is fully anonymous and contains no personally identifiable information.
• Includes only technical crash stack traces, device model, and Android OS version.
• Is processed by Google under their standard privacy framework.
• Can be opted out of via your device's Google account settings under Data & Privacy → App activity.

5

Android Permissions Explained

The App requests only the minimum permissions required for its functionality:

📡
TRANSMIT_IR
Allows the App to use the phone's IR blaster to send signals to your AC unit. This is the core function of the App.
Required
📳
VIBRATE
Powers the haptic feedback when you press remote buttons. Can be turned off in Settings → Vibration Strength → Off.
Optional
🌐
INTERNET
Used solely to check for app updates and to open this Privacy Policy page in your browser. No personal data is transmitted.
Limited use

Third-Party Services

7

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected from any user, the App is safe for general audiences.
