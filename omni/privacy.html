Overview & Core Promise

This Privacy Policy explains how Omni Universal AC Remote handles user information. Our architecture is completely offline and privacy-first: we do not collect, store, transmit, monetize, or share any personal data. All remote commands operate locally via your phone's built-in Infrared (IR) emitter.

1. Who We Are

Omni Universal AC Remote is developed and published by Karyanest Softwares (founded by Sourav). We build privacy-focused, offline-first software utilities without cloud dependencies, ad trackers, or subscriptions.

  • Website: https://unicsam.github.io

2. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies solely to the Omni Universal AC Remote mobile application (Package Name: ac.remote) distributed via Google Play Store and official channels.

3. Information We Collect

A. Personal Information

None. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, user accounts, or profile data. No login or registration is required to use any feature of the app.

B. Device Information

None. We do not collect device identifiers, IMEI, advertising IDs, MAC addresses, or IP addresses.

C. Usage & Analytics Data

None. We do not embed analytics SDKs (such as Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics). We do not record remote button presses, temperature choices, or session duration.

D. Location Data

None. The app does not request, access, or track GPS or network location data.

E. Hardware & Sensor Data

The app only requests access to the phone's built-in Infrared (IR) emitter hardware to transmit remote control signals to your air conditioner.

4. How We Use Your Information

Because no personal information is collected, we do not use, profile, or monetize any user data. All configuration settings (such as saved AC brand, temperature unit preference, and dark mode) are stored strictly locally on your device using Android's private SharedPreferences sandbox.

5. Permissions Justification

Google Play requires transparent disclosure of all Android permissions declared in the application manifest:

Permission Usage & Purpose Type
android.permission.TRANSMIT_IR Used to transmit infrared beam pulses from your phone's built-in IR blaster to the air conditioner receiver. Required for remote transmission
android.permission.VIBRATE Provides tactile haptic vibration feedback when buttons are tapped. Can be adjusted in Settings. Optional UX enhancement

6. Third-Party Services & SDKs

The app contains zero third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. It integrates only the official Google Play library for updates:

  • Google Play In-App Update API (play:app-update): Enables checking for app updates directly through Google Play.

Any interactions with Google Play services are governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Data Storage & Retention

All application state preferences are stored locally in private application storage on your device. We do not operate external cloud servers or databases. All local preferences are deleted immediately when you clear app data or uninstall the app from Android Settings.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Omni Universal AC Remote does not collect personal information from children or any other users, in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy.

9. Legal Compliance (GDPR & CCPA)

Because our application operates entirely on-device without collecting, storing, or transmitting personal data, we do not share, sell, or transfer any personal data under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).
