Setup Steps

Step 1
Transfer the APK to your TV
Use whichever method matches your setup:
  • Downloader App (Fastest): Open Downloader on TV, enter the URL above, and click Download.
  • Send Files to TV: Download the APK on your phone and send it wirelessly to your TV.
  • USB Drive: Copy the downloaded APK to a thumb drive and plug it into your TV.
Step 2
Open Once to Confirm Activation
Launch TV Link Companion from your TV Apps drawer. You will see:
COMPANION ACTIVE & READY
Press Return to TV Home. No further configuration is needed.
Step 3
Launch Directly from Your Remote
Tap your app's shortcut inside the TV Link Remote phone app. Your TV will switch to the app immediately.

Technical FAQ

Why is a companion app required for some apps?
On Android TV 11+ and Google TV, OS security policy prevents remote controllers from directly invoking arbitrary package intents without a registered local handler. The TV Link Companion acts as that lightweight handler on the TV.
Does it consume memory or run in the background?
No. The companion has 0 persistent background services, 0 broadcast listeners, and 0 memory consumption while idle. It activates for ~200 milliseconds to start your app, then closes.
What if I do not install the companion?
The TV Link phone remote automatically falls back to opening the app's official TV Store listing after a brief timeout.
