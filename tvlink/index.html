Universal Android TV Remote

A fast, local remote control for your Android TV and Fire TV. Control navigation, type text, trigger voice search, and sync media status — all over your local network.

Features

Built for responsiveness and speed.

Universal Android TV Remote connects locally via standard network protocols, delivering instant response times without cloud servers or user tracking.

Local TV Auto-Discovery

Scans your local network via mDNS to find Android TVs, Google TVs, and Fire TVs instantly. Tap to pair and connect.

Secure Encryption

Uses official pairing certificates to establish an encrypted TLS connection directly between your phone and your TV.

Instant Touchpad & D-Pad

Navigate layouts using a physical D-pad design or a large touch gesture swipe area. Adjustable touch sensitivities.

Sync Keyboard Entry

Type passwords, searches, or URLs on your phone's keyboard and see them sync instantly on your TV screen.

Voice Commands

Press the microphone button to dictate commands, launch apps, or perform voice search directly on your Android TV.

Media Session Syncing

Automatically syncs volume levels, mute status, and actively playing Chromecast metadata back to your phone's screen.

How it works

Three steps to connect.

Ensure your phone and your TV are connected to the same Wi-Fi network before getting started.

1

Scan for TVs

Open the app. It will automatically scan your local Wi-Fi network to detect any visible Android or Google TVs.

2

Enter Pairing PIN

Select your TV. A 6-character pairing PIN code will appear on your TV screen. Enter it on your phone to pair securely.

3

Control Your Screen

Once paired, you are connected. You can control the D-pad, swipe, haptic feedbacks, and type text instantly.
