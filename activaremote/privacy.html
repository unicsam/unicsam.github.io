Privacy Policy

Activa BLDC Fan Remote (com.karyanest.activaremote)
Zero Data Collected — 100% Offline Hardware IR
Summary

Activa BLDC Fan Remote is built with privacy as an absolute priority. The application does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, identifiers, or analytics. It operates purely as an offline Infrared (IR) transmitter using your phone's built-in IR blaster hardware.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) or device information. Specifically:

  • No account creation or registration is required.
  • No device IDs, IP addresses, location data, or telemetry are recorded.
  • No usage analytics or crash tracking SDKs are embedded.
  • No third-party advertising networks are used — the app is 100% free and ad-free forever.

2. Permissions Requested

Activa BLDC Fan Remote only requests standard Android permissions strictly necessary for its functionality:

  • TRANSMIT_IR: Allows the app to access your device's built-in infrared emitter to transmit fan control signals (Speed 1-6, Boost, Nature, Smart, Timers, Light, Reverse).
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Optional, Android 13+): Used exclusively to show the lightweight quick-control remote in your notification drawer if enabled. This can be turned off at any time in Settings.

3. Offline Operation

All IR transmission codes are stored locally on your device and sent directly via hardware. No internet connection is required for remote control operations.

4. Google Play Services

The application utilizes official Google Play Core libraries for in-app ratings (Play In-App Review) and critical version updates (Play In-App Update). Any interaction with Google Play services is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Voluntary Support / Donations

Donations through UPI (Unified Payments Interface) are completely optional and voluntary. When initiating a payment, the app launches your installed UPI banking apps (such as Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm). No financial credentials or transaction details are accessed or processed by our application.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
