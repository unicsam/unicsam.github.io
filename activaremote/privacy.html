Overview & Core Promise

This Privacy Policy explains how Activa BLDC Fan Remote handles user information. Our fundamental architecture is completely offline and privacy-first: we do not collect, store, transmit, monetize, or sell any of your personal data or usage metrics. All remote commands operate locally via your phone's built-in Infrared (IR) emitter.

2. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies solely to the Activa BLDC Fan Remote mobile application (Package Name: com.karyanest.activaremote) distributed via Google Play Store and official channels.

3. Information We Collect

A. Personal Information

None. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, account credentials, payment info, or biometric data. No account creation or registration is required to use any feature of the app.

B. Device Information

None. We do not collect or record device serial numbers, IMEI, advertising IDs (GAID/AAID), MAC addresses, or IP addresses.

C. Usage & Analytics Data

None. We do not embed analytics SDKs (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or Flurry). We do not record button clicks, session duration, screen views, or user interaction patterns.

D. Location Data

None. The app does not request, access, monitor, or track GPS, network location, or Wi-Fi triangulation data.

E. Permissions-Based Data

The app only requests access to the device's hardware Infrared (IR) emitter. No access is requested or used for camera, microphone, contacts, storage, SMS, photos, or phone state.

4. How We Collect Information

Because the app does not collect personal data, no data collection mechanisms (such as web forms, tracking cookies, background daemons, or server APIs) are present in the app.

5. How We Use Your Information

Since no personal information is collected, we do not use, profile, or monetize any user data. All configuration settings (such as chosen UI theme or notification preference) are stored strictly locally on your device using Android's private SharedPreferences sandbox.

6. Permissions Justification

Google Play requires transparent disclosure of all Android permissions declared in the manifest:

Permission Usage & Purpose Mandatory / Optional
android.permission.TRANSMIT_IR Used to transmit infrared frequency signals from your phone's built-in IR blaster to the Activa BLDC ceiling fan receiver (speeds 1–6, Boost, Power, Timers, Nature, Smart, Reverse). Mandatory for remote functionality
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Used on Android 13+ (API 33+) to display the optional quick-access remote buttons in the notification drawer while the app is active. Can be disabled anytime in Settings. Optional

Any interactions with Google Play services are governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share user data with any third parties. Because we do not collect any personal data, no user information can be disclosed to advertisers, data brokers, law enforcement, or external entities.

9. Data Storage & Security

All application state preferences (such as light/dark mode preference and notification enable switch) are stored locally in private application storage on your device. We do not maintain any external cloud servers or remote databases.

10. Data Retention Policy

Because no personal data is transmitted to external servers, we retain no user data. Local preferences are retained on your device solely for the duration of the app's installation and are deleted immediately when you uninstall the app or clear app storage in Android Settings.

11. User Rights & Choices
