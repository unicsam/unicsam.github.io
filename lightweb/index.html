Quick Reference

TV Remote Button Cheat Sheet

You do not need a physical keyboard or mouse. LightWeb is engineered around standard TV remote controls.

D-PAD ARROWS

Move Cursor / Scroll

Tap for micro-nudging links; hold for smooth acceleration across the screen.

DPAD CENTER

Left Click & Menu

Short tap clicks the hovered element. Long-press opens the TV Context Menu.

BACK KEY

Back / Navigation HUD

Navigates back one page in history. Pressing again brings up the 3-Space Navigation HUD.

MENU KEY

Quick Browser Controls

Opens Zoom, Extensions, Cursor Speed, Bookmarks, and Settings drawer instantly.

PLAY / PAUSE

Media Playback

Directly toggles HTML5 video playback without needing to hover video controls.

FAST FWD / REW

Seek Video ±10s

Skips forward or rewinds media playback by 10 seconds smoothly.

Setup

How to Install on Android TV

Method 1: Sideload via Downloader App (Easiest)
  1. Install the free Downloader by AFTVnews app from the Google Play Store or Amazon Appstore on your TV.
  2. Open Downloader, enter the direct APK URL or short code for LightWeb.
  3. Once downloaded, click Install. If prompted, grant "Install Unknown Apps" permission to Downloader in your TV Settings.
Method 2: Sideload via Send Files to TV (SFTV)
  1. Download the LightWeb APK onto your Android phone or PC.
  2. Install Send Files to TV on both your TV and your phone/PC.
  3. Transfer the APK over local Wi-Fi, then open it with any file manager on your TV to install.
Method 3: Install via ADB (Developers & Power Users)
Enable Developer Options and Network Debugging on your Android TV, then execute from your PC:

adb connect <TV_IP_ADDRESS>:5555
adb install -r LightWeb-Full-Universal.apk
