Privacy Policy

Application: LightWeb Browser (com.lightweb) • Effective Date: September 2026

Status Notice: LightWeb Browser is currently under active pre-release development and testing. It has not been publicly commercialized.

1. Our Core Privacy Philosophy

LightWeb is built specifically as a zero-bloat, lightweight web browser for TVs and connected devices. Your privacy is protected by default:

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, device identifiers, IP addresses, or browsing history.
  • No Tracking & No Profiling: We do not track websites you visit, search queries you make, or build advertising profiles.
  • No Advertisements: LightWeb contains zero ads, third-party ad networks, or commercial adSDK integrations.
  • Offline & Local Storage: All bookmarks, speed dials, and tab history are stored exclusively on your local device hardware and are never synchronized to our servers.

2. Crash Diagnostics & Anonymous Stability Data

To identify and fix unexpected app crashes across varied Android TV hardware chips, LightWeb incorporates the Google Firebase Crashlytics SDK.

  • What is collected on a crash: Anonymous stack traces, operating system version, device model, and crash log events.
  • What is NEVER collected: Web URLs, form input, keystrokes, personal identity, or sensitive browser content.
  • Purpose: Strictly debugging and resolving application memory leaks, engine panics, or rendering crashes.

3. Web Content & Mozilla GeckoView Engine

LightWeb utilizes Mozilla's open-source GeckoView engine to render web pages. When you visit third-party websites, those sites may operate their own privacy policies, cookies, and network requests. LightWeb includes native ad-blocking and tracking protection rules to minimize third-party cross-site trackers by default.

4. Data Deletion & User Control

You have 100% control over all data stored on your device:

  • You can wipe all history and speed dials directly from the in-app Settings menu (Clear History & Cache).
  • Uninstalling LightWeb or clearing app data from Android System Settings instantly and permanently destroys all locally saved data.
