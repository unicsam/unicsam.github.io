Privacy Policy

Net Blocker by Karyanest Softwares

Effective date: August 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Net Blocker application ("the App") handles information. Net Blocker is developed by Karyanest Softwares as an offline-first, privacy-focused application. By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

Core Privacy Commitment: Net Blocker operates 100% on your device. We do not operate any backend servers, and we do not collect, monitor, store, log, or transmit any user network traffic, browsing history, or personal data.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal data or network traffic. Net Blocker is a local firewall tool. The App does not collect, record, or track your browsing activity, websites visited, DNS queries, packet payloads, device identifiers, advertising IDs, or personal information.

No user account, login, email address, or registration is required to use the App.

2. How the Local Firewall and VPN Service Work

Net Blocker utilizes Android's VpnService API exclusively as an on-device local firewall to manage network access for applications installed on your device.

  • Local Processing: All network traffic inspection and filtering happen entirely on your device's local loopback.
  • No Remote Routing: The App does not route your internet traffic through any external proxy or remote VPN servers.
  • Local Rule Enforcement: The VPN interface is used solely to sinkhole or allow network sockets for specific applications according to your configured Blacklist or Whitelist rules.

3. Permissions and Their Purpose

Net Blocker requests the following permissions to deliver its core firewall functionality. Each permission is used strictly as described:

  • VpnService (BIND_VPN_SERVICE): Required by the Android OS to create an on-device VPN interface for intercepting and blocking network connections of user-selected apps.
  • Query All Packages (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES): Required to enumerate and display the list of installed applications on your device so you can view and toggle Wi-Fi and Mobile Data blocking rules for individual apps. This package data is read locally and never leaves your device.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE): Required to maintain continuous, uninterrupted firewall filtering in the background and display an ongoing notification with quick controls (Pause / Resume Protection).
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to display the ongoing firewall status notification in your status bar and provide quick action buttons.
  • Receive Boot Completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Used to automatically restore your configured firewall protection after your device restarts, if enabled.
  • Network State (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, INTERNET): Used locally to detect whether the device is connected to Wi-Fi or Mobile Data to apply separate network blocking rules accurately.

4. Crash Diagnostics & Stability

To identify bugs and maintain stability across diverse Android devices, the App integrates Google Firebase Crashlytics. When an unexpected app crash occurs, Crashlytics may collect anonymous, non-personally identifiable diagnostic data (such as stack traces, device model, operating system version, and timestamp). This data is used solely to diagnose and fix technical crashes.

5. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The App contains no advertising SDKs, no user tracking trackers, and no data broker integrations. We do not sell, trade, or monetize user data.

6. Data Storage & Retention

Your firewall rules and application preferences are stored locally on your device using encrypted Room database and Android Jetpack DataStore. This data never leaves your device and is completely removed if you uninstall the App or clear App storage via Android Settings.

7. Children's Privacy

Net Blocker does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The App does not solicit data from minors.
