Privacy Policy

Android TV ADB Remote (com.tvlink.adb.remote)
Last Updated: September 30, 2026

Android TV ADB Remote is designed as a direct local utility. Remote commands, navigation gestures, and settings are processed entirely on your local Wi-Fi network and never leave your home environment.

This Privacy Policy explains how Karya Nest Softwares (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles device permissions, user data, and privacy within the Android TV ADB Remote mobile application.

1. Data Safety & Local Processing

  • No Personal Information Collected: We do not collect, store, or sell personal identifiers such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, or geolocation.
  • Direct Local Network Operation: All remote control actions (D-Pad navigation, volume adjustment, media playback, number pad entries, and text typing) are transmitted directly between your phone and your television over your local home network (Wi-Fi). These control signals are processed exclusively in volatile memory and are never transmitted to external cloud servers.
  • Zero Keystroke Logging: Any keyboard input sent to your TV is processed on-the-fly and never stored, logged, or recorded.

2. Permissions and Their Usage

The Application requests only the essential permissions required to deliver reliable TV remote functionality:

  • Local Network & Wi-Fi Access (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE, CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE): Required to discover compatible smart TVs on your home network and send control commands directly to the TV’s network port.
  • Bluetooth Permissions (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH_SCAN, BLUETOOTH_ADVERTISE): Used solely to power on and wake compatible smart TVs from standby sleep mode, and to operate the optional on-screen Air Mouse / trackpad pointer via Bluetooth HID. Bluetooth is never used for location tracking or user profiling.
  • Haptic Vibration (VIBRATE): Used to provide subtle physical touch feedback when tapping remote buttons.
  • No Microphone Access: This application does not require or request microphone or audio recording permissions.

3. Third-Party Services & Diagnostics

To ensure application stability, diagnose unexpected crashes, and support ongoing development, the App integrates standard Google services:

  • Google AdMob: Serves advertisements to support the free version of the application. AdMob may process non-personal device identifiers and advertising telemetry in accordance with Google’s Advertising Policies.
  • Google Firebase (Crashlytics & Analytics): Collects anonymous crash logs, device hardware models, and app performance metrics to help us fix bugs and improve performance. No personally identifiable data is gathered.

4. In-App Purchases & Payments

Any optional upgrades, ad-free access, or Pro feature passes are processed exclusively through Google Play In-App Billing. We do not receive, process, or store credit card numbers, bank details, UPI information, or payment credentials. All billing is handled directly by Google Play.

The Application is a general utility tool and is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
