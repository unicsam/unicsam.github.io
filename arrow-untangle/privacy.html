Overview & Core Promise

This Privacy Policy explains how Arrow Untangle treats your information. Our design philosophy is simple, distraction-free, and private: we do not show third-party ads, and we do not collect, store, transmit, monetize, track, or share any personal data. All gameplay progress and puzzle settings remain strictly on your local device.

1. Who We Are

Arrow Untangle is developed and published by Karya Nest Softwares (founded by Sourav). We create handcrafted, distraction-free mobile games and tools built to run locally on your device without cloud surveillance or unnecessary permissions.

2. Data Collection Policy

Arrow Untangle collects zero personal data. Specifically:

A. Personal Identifiable Information (PII)

None. We do not require account registration, email addresses, phone numbers, names, or profile creation. You can play every level completely anonymously.

B. Financial & Payment Data

None. Arrow Untangle does not collect credit card, banking, or billing details. Any future in-app purchases or tips would be handled entirely through Google Play / Apple In-App Billing systems.

C. Photos, Files & Media

None. The app never requests access to your photos, camera, microphone, internal media storage, or contact lists.

D. Analytics & Telemetry Data

None. The game contains zero behavioral analytics SDKs (such as Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or Amplitude). We do not record screen views, tap coordinates, session durations, or player profiles.

E. Location Information

None. Arrow Untangle never requests or accesses GPS, network-based location, or Wi-Fi triangulation data.

3. Advertising Policy

Arrow Untangle contains zero third-party advertisements. The game does not bundle ad network SDKs (such as Google AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin, ironSource, or Meta Audience Network). There are no pop-up interstitials, banners, video ads, or tracking identifiers (like Google Advertising ID / IDFA).

4. How Your Gameplay Data Is Stored

All game data (including your current level, challenge vs. casual mode, sound/haptic toggles, and color theme) is stored strictly in your device's private local storage (browser sandbox localStorage). Because your device never syncs this gameplay state to external cloud servers, your information is completely protected from remote server leaks or data breaches.

5. Permissions and System Access

Arrow Untangle follows the principle of least privilege and requires minimal system access:

Permission / Feature Usage & Purpose Type
android.permission.VIBRATE Used to generate tactile haptic feedback when rotating arrows, clearing puzzles, or bumping obstacles. This can be toggled off at any time inside the game Settings. Normal
android.permission.INTERNET Standard permission bundled by the underlying native WebView runtime (Capacitor). Arrow Untangle does not transmit personal data, analytics, or background telemetry over the internet. Normal

6. Third-Party Services & Libraries

Arrow Untangle does not integrate third-party marketing, data brokers, or advertising trackers. Any standard operating system interactions (such as system updates or app installs through Google Play or Apple App Store) are governed by the respective platform policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

7. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Resetting Progress: You can reset or change levels directly from the in-game level selector or restart dialog.
  • Complete Deletion on Uninstall: Because all game data is kept inside the app's isolated local sandbox, uninstalling Arrow Untangle from your device immediately and permanently deletes all stored progress.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

Arrow Untangle is a family-friendly procedural untangle puzzle game suitable for audiences of all ages (rated Everyone / 3+). We do not knowingly collect, harvest, or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). The game complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy.

9. International Privacy Compliance (GDPR & CCPA / CPRA)

  • GDPR (European Union & UK): Because we do not collect, process, or transmit personal data to remote servers, no international data transfers or processing operations occur.
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA): We do not collect personal information, and we do not sell, share, or monetize consumer information for monetary or valuable consideration.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this policy if new gameplay features or legal standards require it. Any updates will be published directly to this page with a revised effective date.
