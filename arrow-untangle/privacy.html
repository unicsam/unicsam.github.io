This Privacy Policy describes how Karya Nest Softwares handles user information for the mobile application Arrow Untangle. The app is built with an offline-first architecture and does not collect, record, transmit, or share personal data.

1. Information Collection and Use

Arrow Untangle does not collect any personal data or usage metrics:

  • No Personal Information: We do not ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, accounts, or user profiles.
  • No Financial Data: The application does not collect payment details, credit card numbers, or billing addresses.
  • No Location Data: The application does not request, access, or track GPS coordinates or network-based location.
  • No Device Files: The application does not access your photos, media gallery, camera, microphone, or address book.
  • No Analytics SDKs: There are no analytics frameworks (such as Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or Amplitude) integrated into the app.

2. Advertising

Arrow Untangle does not display advertisements. The application contains no third-party ad network libraries (such as Google AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin, or Meta Audience Network) and does not access advertising identifiers (like Google Advertising ID).

3. Device Permissions

Arrow Untangle requests only the minimal system permissions required to run on Android:

Permission Purpose
android.permission.VIBRATE Generates subtle tactile haptic pulses when rotating or clearing arrows. This can be enabled or disabled in the in-game settings dialog.
android.permission.INTERNET Standard network permission included by the native application wrapper. Arrow Untangle does not transmit personal data, telemetry, or user content over the network.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Game state (such as your current puzzle level, sound and vibration toggles, and casual or challenge game mode) is stored locally on your device using the browser sandbox storage (localStorage).

This data remains on your physical hardware. It is never synchronized to external servers. Uninstalling the application from your device removes all stored level progress and settings.

5. Children's Privacy

Arrow Untangle is a family-safe puzzle game suitable for general audiences of all ages. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

6. Third-Party Services

The application relies solely on standard operating system platforms for distribution and updates (Google Play Store). Platform interactions with Google are governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Policy Updates

We may revise this Privacy Policy when updating game features or compliance guidelines. Any updates will be reflected directly on this page with an updated effective date.
