Features

Built around device-to-device communication.

Game moves and audio travel directly between players without intermediate servers logging your matches.

Encrypted Voice Chat

Audio is streamed directly between players over WebRTC. Memory-only, volatile processing that is never recorded or uploaded to external servers.

Local Wi-Fi Discovery

Automatic discovery over home Wi-Fi or mobile hotspots using Multicast DNS. Play locally in the same room with zero internet access required.

Cryptographic Identity

No registration, phone numbers, or passwords. Your identity is a local cryptographic keypair saved in your device's private sandbox.

Direct Link Invites

Share match invites via WhatsApp or message links with deep linking. Friends join directly into the room with one tap.

Multi-Game Tabletop Suite

Switch between Ludo, 16 Guti, and Tic-Tac-Toe within the same app. Built to support multiple classic board games under one unified P2P engine.

Zero Third-Party Ads

No ad banners, no tracking frameworks, and no advertising identifiers. The board remains clean and private.
