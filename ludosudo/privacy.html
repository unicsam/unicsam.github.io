Summary of Data Safety

Ludo Sudo is designed with a decentralized, peer-to-peer (P2P) architecture. We do not require accounts or logins, we do not operate a centralized database, and we do not store or collect personal information on external servers. Real-time in-game voice chat is transmitted directly between players and is never recorded or stored.

2. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies solely to the Ludo Sudo mobile application (Package Name: ludo.sudo) distributed via Google Play Store and official channels.

3. Information We Collect

A. Personal Information

None. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, user accounts, passwords, or profile credentials. No registration or login is required to play.

B. In-Game Display Name & Preferences

Players may choose a custom display name or roll an in-game gamer tag. This display name and your game settings (such as sound effects and fullscreen mode) are stored strictly locally on your device in Android's private SharedPreferences sandbox. They are never sent to a remote user database.

C. Real-Time In-Game Voice Chat (Microphone)

The application provides an optional live voice chat feature for multiplayer matches. Audio streams are transmitted directly between players using encrypted WebRTC (DTLS-SRTP) peer-to-peer connections. Voice audio is processed strictly in real time in volatile memory; it is never recorded, saved, transcribed, or uploaded to any server. Media streams terminate immediately when you leave or finish a match.

D. Network & Multiplayer Communications

  • Local Wi-Fi Discovery: To discover nearby games on the same Wi-Fi network, the app uses standard Network Service Discovery (mDNS). No data leaves your local home network.
  • Peer-to-Peer Signaling: When matching online, temporary signaling exchanges network routing data (ICE candidates/IP and port) solely to negotiate the direct P2P link between players.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Lightweight notifications delivered via Google Play Services to notify friends of match invites. We do not store or associate push tokens with personal identities.

4. Android Permissions Justification

Google Play requires transparent disclosure of Android permissions declared in the application manifest:

Permission Usage & Purpose Type
android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO Used exclusively for optional live voice communication during multiplayer matches. Audio is transmitted peer-to-peer and is never recorded or stored. Runtime (Sensitive)
android.permission.MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS Adjusts audio routing between the device loudspeaker, earpiece, or connected Bluetooth headsets during voice chat. Normal
android.permission.INTERNET Enables online peer-to-peer game communication and Firebase push notifications. Normal
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Detects whether device is connected to Wi-Fi or cellular network to optimize game connection. Normal
android.permission.CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE Allows the app to broadcast and receive Multicast DNS (mDNS) packets to discover nearby players on local Wi-Fi. Normal

5. Third-Party Services & SDKs

The application contains zero third-party advertising or tracking SDKs. It integrates only standard Google Play services:

  • Google Play In-App Update API: Checks for critical application updates directly through Google Play.
  • Google Play In-App Review API: Allows players to submit feedback directly to the Play Store.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google LLC): Transmits incoming game invite alerts via Google Play Services.

Interactions with Google Play services are governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Data Storage & Retention

Because Karya Nest does not operate external user databases or cloud servers, no user data is retained remotely. All preferences and display names reside entirely in your phone's sandbox storage. Clearing the application data in your Android Settings or uninstalling the app permanently removes all local data immediately.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

Ludo Sudo is a family-friendly board game suitable for general audiences of all ages. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

8. Legal Compliance (GDPR & CCPA)

Because the app operates on-device and peer-to-peer without collecting, storing, or selling personal data, we do not monetize, share, or process personal data under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).
