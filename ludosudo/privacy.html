This Privacy Policy describes how Karya Nest ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, processes, and protects user information when you install and play our mobile game Ludo Sudo (the "Application" or "Game").

Ludo Sudo is engineered with a strict Privacy-First and Decentralized Peer-to-Peer (P2P) Architecture. We do not require account registration, passwords, email addresses, phone numbers, or social media logins to play. We do not sell user data or track users across external apps and websites.

1. Data Safety & Information Collection

In accordance with Google Play's Data Safety declaration requirements, below is a transparent breakdown of all data handled by Ludo Sudo:

Data Category Specific Data Point Collection Status Purpose & Storage
Personal Info Player Nickname / Display Name Optional (User-set) Stored strictly locally on your device in SharedPreferences. Transmitted peer-to-peer to match participants so opponents can see your name during gameplay. Never stored in a central remote user database.
Microphone / Audio In-Game Voice Chat Stream Ephemeral (P2P) Transmitted in real-time between match players via encrypted WebRTC streams (DTLS-SRTP). Audio is strictly ephemeral and never recorded, saved, transcribed, or stored on any server.
Device & Other IDs Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) Token Collected solely for notifications Used exclusively for peer matchmaking wake-up signals and offline game invite notifications. Exchanged directly with mutual friends or ephemeral matchmaking relays.

Financial / Payment Credit cards, bank details Not Collected The app does not collect or process financial information.
Precise Location GPS coordinates Not Collected Local Wi-Fi discovery uses Android Network Service Discovery (NSD/mDNS) over the local subnet without accessing GPS location.

2. In-Game Voice Chat & Permissions

Ludo Sudo includes an optional real-time voice chat feature for friends and multiplayer opponents. The following permissions are requested exclusively when necessary:

  • android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO: Requested only when you join an active voice-enabled room or tap the in-game microphone. Used solely to capture your voice for real-time transmission to peers in your match.
  • android.permission.MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS: Used to route audio appropriately between your device loudspeaker, earpiece, or connected Bluetooth headsets.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE & android.permission.ACCESS_WIFI_STATE: Used to verify network connectivity and establish local Wi-Fi / LAN multiplayer connections.
  • android.permission.CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE: Used to broadcast and discover nearby games on the local Wi-Fi network using Multicast DNS / NSD.
  • android.permission.WAKE_LOCK: Ensures the game session and audio processing remain active during active gameplay.
Important Privacy Guarantee: Voice conversations are strictly transient and peer-to-peer. Neither Karya Nest nor any third-party records, logs, listens to, or archives player voice conversations. When your match concludes, the WebRTC media stream is immediately destroyed.

3. Account System & Data Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

Ludo Sudo utilizes a serverless, decentralized identity model. You are not forced to register an account with an email address, phone number, or password. Your identity consists of a cryptographically generated cryptographic keypair stored inside your device's protected sandboxed storage.

To adhere fully to Google Play's Account Deletion and User Data Policy, we provide two complete pathways for data erasure:

  1. In-App Deletion Mechanism: Open Settings > Reset & Erase All Player Data in Ludo Sudo. Tapping this will instantly and permanently wipe your player profile, locally stored keys, friend lists, match preferences, and cached tokens from your device storage.
  2. Web Deletion Resource: If you have uninstalled the application or wish to ensure all cached relay/token data is purged, you can visit our dedicated web deletion portal:

7. Security & Encryption in Transit

All communication channels utilized by Ludo Sudo employ industry-standard encryption protocols:

  • P2P Voice Streams are encrypted end-to-end using SRTP (Secure Real-time Transport Protocol) with DTLS (Datagram Transport Layer Security) handshakes.
  • Push signaling and relay channels are protected via TLS 1.3 / HTTPS / WSS.
  • Local identity keys and contact fingerprints are encrypted within the Android sandboxed application directory.
