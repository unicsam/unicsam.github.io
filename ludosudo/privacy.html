Privacy Policy

Application: Ludo Sudo
Package Name: ludo.sudo
Developer: Karya Nest
Effective Date: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Karya Nest handles user information for the mobile application Ludo Sudo. The application is built with a decentralized, peer-to-peer (P2P) architecture. We do not operate a centralized backend server, we do not require account registration, and we do not store or collect personal data on external servers.

1. Information Collection and Handling

Ludo Sudo operates without a central user database:

  • No Account Creation: You do not register with an email address, phone number, password, or social media login.
  • Local Player Profile: Your in-game display name, sound toggles, and game preferences are stored exclusively on your device using Android sandboxed local storage (SharedPreferences). This data remains on your physical hardware and is never synced to any remote database.
  • No Ads or Trackers: The application contains no advertising SDKs, no behavioral tracking frameworks, and does not collect or transmit Android Advertising IDs.

2. Microphone Permission & In-Game Voice Chat

Ludo Sudo includes an optional real-time voice chat feature for multiplayer matches:

  • Permission: The application requests android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO only when you enable or use voice communication during a match.
  • Peer-to-Peer Transmission: Audio is transmitted directly between players' devices using encrypted WebRTC streams (DTLS-SRTP).
  • Zero Recording & Storage: Audio streams are strictly real-time and ephemeral. Voice chat is never recorded, saved, transcribed, or stored on any server. The audio stream terminates immediately when the match ends.

3. Network & Multiplayer Operation

The application uses standard network connections for multiplayer features:

  • Local Wi-Fi Matches: Discovers nearby players on your local home network using Network Service Discovery (mDNS/multicast). Gameplay data stays entirely within your local Wi-Fi router.
  • Online P2P Signaling: Online matches use lightweight, temporary signaling channels to exchange WebRTC network addresses (IP/port) so devices can establish direct peer-to-peer connections. Gameplay moves are transmitted directly between players.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Handled via Google Play Services to deliver match invite notifications. The device token is generated by Google and is used solely to route incoming game alerts.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because Karya Nest does not store personal data or account records on central servers, there is no remote user data to retain or delete. All game state and preferences reside locally on your device. Clearing the app storage in your Android system settings or uninstalling the application permanently deletes all local data immediately.

5. Third-Party Services

The application interacts only with standard Google Play platform components:

  • Google Play In-App Updates & Reviews: Standard operating system dialogs governed by Google. Please refer to the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Children's Privacy

Ludo Sudo is a family-safe board game suitable for players of all ages. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy. We do not collect or solicit personal information from children.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect game feature updates or regulatory changes. Any updates will be posted directly to this page with a revised effective date.
