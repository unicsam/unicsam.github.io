In accordance with Google Play’s User Data & Account Deletion Policy, this page allows users of Ludo Sudo to request complete deletion of their player data and associated identifiers without needing to keep the app installed.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

Because Ludo Sudo uses a decentralized, local-first architecture, you can wipe 100% of your data instantly directly on your device:

  1. Launch Ludo Sudo on your Android phone.
  2. Tap the Settings (Gear Icon) from the Main Menu.
  3. Scroll down to the Data & Privacy section.
  4. Tap "RESET & ERASE ALL PLAYER DATA" and confirm the prompt.

This immediately purges your player nickname, cryptographic identity keypair, saved friend list, audio preferences, and cached tokens from your device sandbox.

Because Ludo Sudo does not maintain centralized user databases, your notification tokens and cached relay entries will be purged within 48 hours. A confirmation email will be delivered to your address.

What Data Is Deleted?

  • Player Profile & Nickname: Removed from local storage and any ephemeral matchmaking cache.
  • Cryptographic Identity: Cryptographic keys generated on the device are permanently destroyed.
  • Friend Lists & Shared Keys: Any mutual friendship tokens stored locally are discarded.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) Tokens: Unregistered from cloud push queues.

Data Retention Period

Because we do not store voice recordings or persistent user activity logs on central servers, no historical gameplay data is retained. Ephemeral matchmaking sessions expire automatically within 15 minutes, and manual requests are verified and confirmed within 48 hours.
